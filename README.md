## ⚙️ How to Setup ClashX (Tutorial)

Even though development has stopped, the **configuration logic is identical to other mainstream Clash clients**.

### 1. Import Your Subscription
1. Click the **ClashX icon** in the top-right menu bar.
2. Go to: `Config` → `Remote Config` → `Manage`.
3. Click the **Add** button.
4. Paste your **ClashX Subscription URL**.
5. Give it a name and click OK. The configuration will download and activate.

---

### 2. Choosing Proxy Modes
Click the menu bar icon and select a mode:
* **Rule (Recommended)** ✅: Traffic is split based on the rules in your configuration.
* **Global**: All traffic goes through the proxy.
* **Direct**: All traffic bypasses the proxy.

You can select specific nodes or groups under the **Proxies** menu.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is ClashX still usable in 2026?
✅ Yes, provided that:
* Your macOS version is compatible (Legacy apps may break in future macOS updates).
* You are using standard protocols (SS / Trojan / Vmess). It does **not** natively support newer protocols like Hysteria2.

### Is ClashX recommended for new users?
⚠️ **Not as a primary tool.**
It is best used as:
* A lightweight backup client.
* A client for older Mac hardware.
* A simple solution for users who already have stable Clash configurations.
